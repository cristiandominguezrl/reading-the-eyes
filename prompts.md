# Prompts

These are the prompts used to generate the chosen photograph of each item. Each prompt is the common photographic specification, then a person description, then an expression description, then a lighting note that differs between the three takes. The target state named inside each prompt was for the researchers' records. The generator was asked for photographs only and was not shown the answer options.

## Common photographic specification

A black and white photograph, tightly cropped to the eye region of one adult: from just above the eyebrows to just below the lower eyelids. Both eyes fully visible, the bridge of the nose visible, no mouth, no hair except where it meets the forehead. Landscape framing, 5:2 width to height. Candid documentary photography, like a crop from a magazine portrait: real skin texture, pores and fine lines, natural light, slight film grain, no retouching, no stylised makeup, no illustration or 3D render look, no text, no border, no watermark. The gaze and the small movements of brows, lids and the muscles around the eyes must carry the mental state on their own, since nothing else of the face is shown. Keep the expression subtle, as caught in a real moment, never acted or exaggerated.
